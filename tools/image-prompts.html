בלוק הסגנון

כלול אוטומטית בכל כפתור העתקה. כאן רק לעיון.

הצגת הבלוק
Documentary construction-site photography, realistic and matter-of-fact, like an
image in a safety-inspection report. Israeli construction site: grey concrete
frame, red-orange scaffolding, yellow excavators, white-grey block walls, strong
Mediterranean daylight with hard shadows.

Neutral, slightly desaturated colours. The single element the scene is about is
clearly visible and well lit, in the middle third of the frame.

Eye-level camera, natural perspective, no fisheye, no drone view, no dramatic
angle. No motion blur.

Nothing is broken, collapsed or on fire, and nobody is injured. This is the
moment before, not after.

16:9 landscape.

No text, no words, no letters, no numbers, no readable signage, no logos, no
brand marks, no watermarks anywhere in the image.

נפילה מפיגום קצה

c1.jpg לייצור

הצגת הפרומפט
A steel frame scaffold standing along the facade of a concrete building under
construction, seen from the side and slightly below, six storeys up.

The top working platform is made of plain wooden boards and is completely open:
no guardrail, no mid-rail and no toe board along its outer edge. The lower
platforms of the same scaffold do have guardrails, so the missing rail on the top
level reads instantly.

Two plasterers in work clothes and helmets stand on the top platform, working on
the wall, both well away from the edge and standing safely.

No safety net under the platform, no harness lines, no people near the open edge.

אבק סיליקה בניסור יבש

c2.jpg לייצור

הצגת הפרומפט
Inside a closed, unventilated concrete floor of a building under construction. One
worker in the middle of the frame cuts a concrete block with a hand-held dry disc
saw resting on a low workbench.

A thick white cloud of dust hangs in the air around him and drifts through the
shafts of light coming from a single window opening. Fine pale dust covers the
floor, the blocks and the surfaces around.

The worker wears a helmet but no respirator. There is no water feed on the saw and
no extraction hose anywhere in the room.

The saw is cutting normally, nothing is broken, the worker is standing upright and
unhurt.

כבל מתח שנחשף בחפירה

c3.jpg לייצור

הצגת הפרומפט
An open narrow trench cut along the boundary of a construction site, seen from
above ground at eye level. The trench is about one metre deep, with loose earth
piled beside it.

In the side wall of the trench, clearly visible, runs a thick black electrical
cable, partly uncovered by the digging, with earth still clinging to it. No
warning tape and no marker above it.

A yellow excavator stands close to the trench with its bucket lowered near the
same line. Two workers stand at the far end of the trench, upright and at a
distance, tidying the edge.

The cable is intact and undamaged. No sparks, no fire, no one touching the cable.

קריסת תבנית ביציקת תקרה

c4.jpg לייצור

הצגת הפרומפט
A ceiling formwork deck on the third floor of a building under construction, seen
from below and to the side. The deck is carried by a forest of steel props.

Several props in the centre of the frame are visibly loose and not tight against
the beams above, and one stands slightly tilted. The deck above them sags very
slightly, a shallow dip that is noticeable against the straight line of the beams
beside it.

Above the deck, a concrete pump hose is pouring wet concrete. Workers in helmets
stand on the deck near the pour, and a few more stand on the floor below.

Nothing has collapsed. The formwork is still standing, the concrete is flowing
normally, nobody is falling or hurt.

חנות עם תקרת עץ נמוכה

c5.jpg לייצור

הצגת הפרומפט
The interior of a finished retail shop, about 400 square metres, photographed from
one corner at eye level.

The ceiling is a continuous surface of dark wooden slats, and it is clearly low:
it sits just above the tops of the tall display units, and the whole space feels
compressed under it.

Open shopping floor with clothing racks and shelves, a few customers walking
between them, calm and ordinary. Warm downlights are recessed in the wooden
ceiling.

There are no sprinklers and no smoke vents on the ceiling. No fire, no smoke, no
alarm, nothing dramatic: an ordinary shopping afternoon.
